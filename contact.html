Frequently Asked Questions

Quick answers to common inquiries

How do I place an order?

You can place an order by calling us, visiting our office, sending an email, or through WhatsApp. We offer flexible payment terms and delivery options.

Do you offer free soil testing?

Yes! We provide free soil testing and analysis to determine the best fertilizer blend for your specific soil conditions.

What is your delivery timeframe?

We offer same-day delivery in Okara and next-day delivery across Punjab depending on order size and location.

Do you offer bulk discounts?

Yes! We provide competitive bulk pricing for large orders. Contact our sales team for customized quotes.

Are your products certified?

Absolutely! All our fertilizers are government certified and meet international quality standards for purity and nutrient content.

Can you provide on-farm consultation?

Yes! Our agronomists offer free farm visits for consultation. We assess your soil and provide personalized recommendations.

Can't Find Your Answer?

Our expert team is ready to help. Reach out directly for any questions.
