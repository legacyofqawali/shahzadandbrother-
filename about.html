About Shahzad & Brothers

Dedicated to Agricultural Excellence Since 2013

Our Story

From Vision to Agricultural Impact

Shahzad & Brothers Fertilizer was founded in 2013 with a simple but powerful vision: to revolutionize agriculture in Punjab by providing farmers with premium-quality fertilizers and expert guidance.

What started as a small local business has grown into one of the most trusted fertilizer suppliers in the region. Our founders, Shahzad sahib and his brothers, understood the challenges farmers faced in sourcing quality inputs and getting reliable agricultural advice.

Today, we serve over 5,000 farmers across Punjab, delivering more than 100 different fertilizer and seed products. Our commitment to quality, reliability, and customer service has made us the preferred choice for farmers who want to maximize their crop yields and ensure sustainable agriculture.

We believe that the future of agriculture depends on providing farmers with the best tools and knowledge. That's why we continue to invest in our facilities, expand our product range, and train our team to provide exceptional service.

Our Mission

To empower farmers with premium agricultural inputs and expert knowledge, enabling them to achieve sustainable crop production, increase yields, and improve their livelihoods through quality fertilizers and personalized agricultural consultation.

Our Vision

To become the most trusted and innovative fertilizer supplier in Pakistan, recognized for our commitment to quality, customer service, and sustainable agricultural practices that benefit farmers and the environment.
